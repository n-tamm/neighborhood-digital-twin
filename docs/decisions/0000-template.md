# Decision: Short title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** names or workstream
- **Replaces:** optional prior decision

## Context

What problem requires a decision? Include the constraints and evidence that matter.

## Decision

State the choice directly.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Describe expected benefits, tradeoffs, migration work, and what would cause the team to revisit the decision.
